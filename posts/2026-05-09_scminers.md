_\*This trip occurred on May 8, 2026._

Skylands Stadium in Augusta, NJ was built in 1994 for a New York-Penn League team that eventually left in 2005[^1]. An independent team started up the following year in the Can-Am League and then spent a few years there until folding in 2011. In 2015, the Sussex County Miners (i.e. the current team) were founded as a member of the Can-Am League and have been there since; they won a Can-Am League title in 2018 before moving to the Frontier League with the other surviving Can-Am teams when that league folded in 2020.

I’ll be honest: I kinda like the charm of this place. Sure, it’s a bit of a hike and in a SUPER rural area (which lends itself to seeing certain...things), but the park has this “barn” like feel that is cool. Plus, there’s a barbecue joint outside the stadium (and inside), the team store is called the “Company Store”, and parking is free!

Most of the concession stands are “isolated” from the field due to the fact that, similar to places like [Heritage Financial Park](2025-08-12_hvrenegades), seats are built over top of them. In fact, a lot of what I've said about that place applies, which makes sense given that the two stadiums were opened in the same year in the same league. However, some of the more "luxury" features in the Hudson Valley are more or less absent here - though that might be due to the park not having an affiliated team since 2005 (i.e. less reason to make renovations to keep an MLB team happy in 2020).

Much like the other Frontier League stops on this blog, I’d already been here LONG before this site existed - once in 2022, and again in 2024. However, I did NOT make a visit in 2025; there was one date that I almost came here, but something came up that prevented me from leaving[^2].

This year, I was originally thinking of going to a game in June, but due to other games I want to see that weekend, it became unlikely. However, with the rest of the Miners' schedule looking "unfriendly" based on my calendar for this summer, I ended up deciding to move my visit up and check out a Frontier League home opener for the [second day in a row](2026-05-08_rocboulders). Once again, I decided to get a front row seat - and this one actually ended up being a bit less than the front row seat I got for the Boulders game (and that's BEFORE factoring in parking)!

### MERCH

Before I continue, I should mention the fact that the Miners are owned by the group that owned the New Jersey Jackals until this year. If you recall what I said about the Jackals' old online store, the same applies here - it’s a spiritwear store that doesn’t sell a lot of the standard baseball fare and/or “official” team stuff (and I've recently learned that there are A LOT of smaller teams that seemingly go this route nowadays[^3]).

Anyway, I mentioned it earlier, but the team store here is called “The Company Store” as a reference to the mining industry. Between visits in 2022 and 2024, there were a few changes to it - the most notable being that they moved the entrance INSIDE the park in 2024 (at least during games - there’s still a door outside the gates, but I have no clue if the store has non-game hours).

I mentioned how a few fans at the Boulders game yesterday had stitched jerseys despite the team not selling them in their team store. Well, here's the thing: I have a stitched Miners jersey that I got from their store in 2022! I'm not fully sure if the team sells replica jerseys, but when I was here in 2024, the jerseys they had were all fully sublimated.

Outside of the standard hats and jerseys (and other things), the team sells a lot of interesting things: I bought a replica championship ring from their 2018 Can-Am League championship[^4] back in 2022, and in 2024, they were selling Harry Potter wands (I think they were leftovers from “Harry Potter Night”).
