### _[2026: New Venue #34](2026newvenuelist)_

You may have heard of Quinnipiac University as a school known for its polling, but Quinnipiac also has a very interesting (men's) hockey team: similar to fellow Connecticut schools UConn and [Sacred Heart](2025-12-31_sacredhearthockey_mens), they were a lower level program before moving to D-I, and were in what is now Atlantic Hockey America before moving to the ECAC in the early 2000s. Since 2013, they’ve largely dominated the ECAC, winning its regular season title eight times, making the NCAA tournament in each of those seasons, and winning a national championship in 2023 (don’t ask about their number of official conference titles however). Because they’ve become such a powerhouse in the ECAC, some have advocated for them to jump conferences again and join Hockey East; I don’t know how the school feels about the prospects, but I’m not sure I can see it happening anytime soon.

Similar to Sacred Heart just down the highway (which is technically NOT the Merritt Parkway - at least not by the time you get to Quinnipiac), their arena is not on the university's main campus, but a little bit west of it. Unlike Sacred Heart, however, it’s not within a secondary campus; from what I could tell, the arena is just surrounded by dorms. It was opened in 2007 after the hockey team spent quite some time at a nearby community rink (WELL off campus), and similar to [Holy Cross](2025-10-21_holycrosshockey_mens), it has fully separate facilities for hockey and basketball adjacent to one another (which share a singular common lobby)[^1].

The physical arena itself isn't entirely unique - a somewhat basic seating bowl and nothing on the "concourses" at the top of the bowl (as the concessions). One thing that is commonly cited as a "knock" against the arena: the large, concrete walls which are mostly undecorated (save for banners).

Given Quinnipiac's status as a college hockey power, it's only natural that someone like me who would want to check them out. However, they're two hours away from both where I am currently AND Boston, so the logistics of trying to go to a game are always tricky (unless I REALLY want to be driving up/down the Merritt Parkway late at night). Thankfully, there are several "midday-ish" games on their schedule (4 PM), so even though "it does get late early out there" during hockey season (i.e. winter), it's not TOO bad (just be wary of towns with speed cameras...)[^3]. Given that the entire lower bowl was one uniform price ($30 - which isn't too bad for a top team like Quinnipiac), I went back and forth on which seat I wanted before settling on...

### MERCH

Much like Sacred Heart, my first visit to the school was a quick stop at the campus bookstore to check out if they had jerseys (though I have COMPLETELY forgotten when this was - but I DEFINITELY visited the two bookstores on the same drive). I should mention that unlike my initial Sacred Heart visit, there were “replica” jerseys in the bookstore (i.e. not quite what the players wear, but somewhat close) and parking was easier to find (though the bookstore wasn't in many respects).
