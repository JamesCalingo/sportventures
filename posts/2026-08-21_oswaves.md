### [SUMMER OF BASEBALL 2023: STOP 8](2026-08-16_summerofbaseball2023)

Truth be told, the Waves are one of the reasons I was super hesitant to post my "old" baseball posts from 2023 for several reasons:

1. There's not much to say about their history: they were founded in 2013 and haven't won a championship. They've been to the finals twice (in back to back years), but were swept both times by the same team.

2. They play in a public park that's fairly similar to [Danbury](2026-07-24_dbywesterners) but without a nearby school building (though it apparently opened in 1938 according to the town it's in[^1]). When I visited, there wasn't a ton to speak about: a small and old concession stand, plus a parking lot that was often rather full when I arrived (close to game time) and SUPER limited bleacher seating (in short: SUPER lowkey).

3. The field is getting a MASSIVE facelift right now with a new bleacher set up and concessions building, so it's likely that if I ever find myself back here, it'll be a VERY different experience. Again, part of why these are going up now is because revisits in the future are INCREDIBLY up in the air (especially as many of the places - this included - are quite remote), so I'll just have to see what happens from afar (though the renderings I saw look impressive).

4. A neat segue into what might just be the most common word in these posts:

### MERCH

Back in the day, tickets and merchandise were sold at the same table just outside the field in the parking lot. From my visits, I got the impression that thye team apparently doesn’t order a lot of hats, as the times I’ve gone have featured very few hats, and the biggest one I’ve seen (outside of a special All-Star hat) is two sizes smaller than what I typically get but somehow fits fine (Ocean State is one of a few teams in New England that exclusively orders fitted hats instead of flexfits).

Given the scope of the upcoming renovation, it'll be interesting to see how they handle merchandise in the future (as - in case you somehow missed it earlier - the renovation is adding a whole new building).
