### _[26 IN 26 STOP 20](/26in26list)_

*\*This trip occurred on June 10, 2026. For more on USL2, [here's an overview](usl2_overview).*

Cedar Stars are, from what I can tell, once part of not only a youth academy, but also a larger soccer program in general known as "Rush": there are at least two other teams in USL2 that are also members - both in the same division as [Vermont Green](2025-08-21_vgfc). Information on the team is a bit limited, but indications are that the USL2 side started up in 2019, and that they have not won a national championship nor have they ever participated in the U.S. Open Cup. 

Two other notes about the team:

1. The team was known as "Cedar Stars Rush" for a while, but when I went to check the team’s 2026 schedule, they had seemingly dropped the "Rush" from their name and are now just "Cedar Stars".
2. They're one of five Metropolitan Division clubs to field a USLW team; one of the other four is [Morris Elite](2026-06-05_morriselite) (plus there's unaffiliated [Paisley Athletic](2026-06-09_paisleyathletic)), but I only have plans to see one of the other three teams.

That said, what is VERY certain (otherwise this post wouldn't exist) is that they play at the soccer field of Fairleigh Dickinson University’s Teaneck campus (which literally does not have a name apparently). I feel like there’s not much to say about FDU, but the school’s basketball team is best known for pulling off a 16-1 upset over Purdue in the NCAA basketball tournament a few years ago (one of only three to have ever happened - men or women).

When the USL2 schedule came out, I had originally penciled in the team for a June 6th visit. However, partially due to Teaneck being MUCH closer to home than [New Rochelle, NY](2026-06-07_westchesterflames), I decided to make the effort to squeeze both teams into my schedule. In fact, part of the reason I knew about this specific game was because the visiting team is...the Westchester Flames!
