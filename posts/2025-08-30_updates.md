So now I've posted about every sporting event I attended in 2025...so far. Of course, this isn't the end for this blog, but I did want to take a little bit of time to sort a few things out.

First, if you haven't seen some posts since the day they went up, you may not have seen the photos I added to them. I believe every post on this blog except one (the MLW post) now has at least one photo, and a couple of them do have more. Most of them are from game action itself (and/or some pregame shot of the field), but a few of them are of other things due to me not having any good photos to post (or edit for posting). I will say that all of them are photos I took, and if they end up on a Google image search somehow, then maybe I'll get some more visitors...

Second, I don't plan on doing this TOO often, but I did go back and edit a few things in the already published posts. There's a small update of a thought I had regarding the Reading game that I forgot about when writing the post, but the most notable edit I made is that there's now an extra part of my Banana Ball post involving a trip to Philadelphia back at the end of July.

Finally, I DO want to mention something regarding the Savannah Bananas K Club (I DID say it was probably going to be important in getting Banana Ball tickets/good seats after all). I realize that it's not all that important in the grand scheme of things, but it's at the forefront of my mind, doesn't feel right to edit into the post (as it is fairly time sensitive), and might be important if you're trying to get in this year.

A few days ago, I checked the K Club website and it said that the 2026 K Club interest list was closed - if you want to sign up for it but missed the boat (so to speak), you'll have to wait for 2027. That alone wouldn't warrant writing this part of the update - however...

On Thursday, I looked at my email and saw a message regarding the K Club. Apparently, getting in is now application based (or maybe it's always been and I forgot), and part of said application asks whether or not you know any current K Club members (y'know, like...me). This is interesting to me, as it seems to indicate that despite their best efforts, the Bananas are still running into the problem of scalpers in the K Club (i.e. the reason the rules surrounding K Club tickets kept changing).

That's all for now. I'll probably delete this in a week or so as the blog transitions to being more "live" (i.e. posts about events a day or two after I've been to them). I am planning another "content catch-up" soon, however, so stay tuned for that.
