Happy October everyone! With the baseball postseason starting yesterday (and the Minor League Baseball season LONG done by now), I feel like it’s time to shift gears.

Back in 2023, I was living in Massachusetts, and there were an ABUNDANCE of hockey games that I went to back then (as I’ve mentioned in [a prior post](2025-08-28_nysirens), hockey is the sport I most like to watch in the winter). I wrote my thoughts and feelings about MOST of the arenas I went to back then[^1]; however, much like this year’s postings (pre-August), I never posted them anywhere...until now.

I’ve decided to dredge up my old hockey posts (specifically college hockey, as these are all about college teams[^2]) and post them here. However, I’m not going to be doing a daily thing as I don’t have as many posts: instead, I’m going to be posting these on the date of each school’s 2025 home opener...mostly. There are a few dates where two schools have their home opener on the same day, but I don’t want to do more than one a day, so I’ll post them back-to-back.

I did try to update these to my "new" 2025 standards, so while they are largely faithful to how I left them in 2024, there are some notable differences: for example, I didn't have a dedicated "merch" section back then, and I also tried to discuss dining options if arena food wasn't your thing (as some of these buildings really don't have good concession options/are rather overpriced). Also, a few of these posts are going to cover multiple visits - but were largely written and updated back then, so there aren't going to be major changes (the last game I attended at any of these places was in the fall of 2024, and I currently don't have any solid plans to visit any of them at the moment).

There are two schools I wrote about back in 2023-24, however, that will not be going up during this time. One of them is UMass Lowell, as [I’ve already posted about the arena as I visited it this year](2025-08-29_bosfleet). The other one I’m not going to say now, but if you’re a true hockey fan, you’ll probably figure it out rather soon. If not, you’ll see when the post about them goes up.

There's also one other team I wrote about back then that I probably won’t be posting about this month, but I MAY get to them in the near future. Part of the reason I’m not posting is because they moved after my visit, and I’d like to try and get to their new home before posting about them (I should also note they’re not an NCAA team).

One thing I will note before wrapping up this intro: some things in these posts are “direction based” in an attempt to explain things a little better. These directions are based on the view you get if you were watching the game on television; the side closest to the cameras is “south”, to the left is “west”, to the right is “east”, and the side furthest from the camera is “north”.

Also, a few of these buildings have special names for their rinks. I’ve noted which places do this by placing the name of the RINK in parentheses before the (common) arena name.

(One final note: I've gone and updated a few older posts again. I realize I shouldn't be doing that, but I can't help myself...)
